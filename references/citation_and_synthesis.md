# Citation And Synthesis

## Citation Gap Rules

Add or request a source when a sentence includes:

- 研究表明 / 数据显示 / 学者认为 / 文献指出
- numerical data, rankings, rates, dates, or policy claims
- comparison between regions, groups, models, or theories
- causal claims such as "导致", "促进", "显著影响"

## Synthesis Pattern

Weak:

> A 学者认为 X。B 学者认为 X。C 学者认为 X。

Better:

> 现有研究普遍将 X 视为影响 Y 的关键因素，但侧重点不同：A 强调机制，B 从数据层面验证了影响方向，C 则指出该关系受 Z 条件限制。因此，本文在已有研究基础上进一步关注……

## Close Paraphrase Warning

Changing word order or synonyms is not enough. If the idea, structure, and evidence come from a source, cite it. If the wording is distinctive, quote it or rewrite as synthesis with citation.

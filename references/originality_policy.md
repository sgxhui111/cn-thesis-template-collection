# Policy And Boundaries

This skill supports ethical academic writing. It does not help users evade AI detectors, plagiarism systems, CNKI checks, Turnitin, iThenticate, or school integrity review.

## Reframe Required

If the user asks for:

- 降低 AI 率
- 绕过 AIGC 检测
- 规避查重
- 洗稿
- 帮我改到检测不出来

Respond by reframing:

> 我不能帮助规避检测，但可以帮你做合规的原创性提升：检查重复风险、补充引用、重组论证、减少模板化表达，并生成可解释的修改记录。

## Allowed

- Identify repetition and close paraphrase risk.
- Identify missing citations and unsupported claims.
- Improve paragraph logic and clarity.
- Suggest how to add the student's own evidence, data, cases, and interpretation.
- Rewrite with citation placeholders when the source is missing.
- Produce a revision log.

## Not Allowed

- Promise lower AI score, lower duplication score, or detection avoidance.
- Remove citation obligations.
- Fabricate sources, data, experiments, survey results, or page numbers.
- Transform copied text into uncited paraphrase.
- Encourage submission of text the student cannot explain.
